SQL challenges

Beginner — count customers by province

Use COUNT and GROUP BY on customers.

SELECT province, COUNT(*) FROM customers GROUP BY province;
Intermediate — revenue by product category

Join products and order_items and aggregate quantity times unit_price.

SELECT p.category, SUM(i.quantity*i.unit_price) AS revenue FROM products p JOIN order_items i ON p.id=i.product_id GROUP BY p.category;
Advanced — rank customers by spending

Aggregate spending in a CTE, then apply DENSE_RANK over descending totals. See the customer ranking example.
